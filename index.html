How Invisalign Works, in Plain Words

Invisalign starts with a scan of your teeth, which makes a 3D map of your mouth. From that map, the team plans a set of clear trays, each one a tiny bit different from the last. You wear each tray for about two weeks, then switch to the next one, and the teeth move a little at a time. You take the trays out to eat, drink anything but water, and brush your teeth. You wear them most of the day and night, about 20 to 22 hours, so they can do their job. Invisalign or Braces? How to Pick

Both can straighten teeth, but they fit different lives. Invisalign trays are hard to spot, they come out for meals and photos, and they do not have wires that poke. Braces stay on all the time, which can help kids who might lose a tray, and they can fix some tough bite problems that trays cannot. Cost, time, and how much care you want to do at home all play a part. The team will look at your bite, your teeth, and your habits, then talk about both choices side by side. There is no single right answer for everyone, and the best pick is the one you will stick with to the end.

A Day in the Life With Trays

A day with trays is simple once you get used to it. You put them in after breakfast, take them out for lunch, brush, and put them back in. You do the same at dinner, and you clean the trays with a soft brush and water. You may feel a bit of pressure for a day or two after a new tray, which means the teeth are moving. You can still play sports, sing, and talk, and most people will not notice the trays at all. If you lose a tray, call the office and do not skip ahead to the next one without asking. A Invisalign dentist Palm Beach Gardens team member can tell you whether to wear the last tray, the next tray, or come in for a new one.

How Long, and What It Costs

Most Invisalign plans take 6 to 18 months, based on how much the teeth need to move. Simple crowding can take less time, while a bite that needs a lot of work can take more. The team gives you a time plan at the scan visit, and they check your progress at regular visits. Cost depends on how many trays you need and how long the plan runs. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most people feel a bit of pressure for a day or two after a new tray, which means the teeth are moving. That feeling fades fast, and the team will tell you what care helps most if a tray rubs your cheek.

How long do I wear each tray?

You wear each tray for about two weeks, then switch to the next one, and you wear them about 20 to 22 hours a day. The team checks your progress at regular visits and tells you if you need to wear a tray a bit longer.

Can I eat with the trays in?

Take the trays out to eat and drink anything but water. Food can stain a tray, and chewing with a tray in can crack it. Brush your teeth before you put the trays back in, so food does not get trapped under them.

Is Invisalign just for teens?

No, many adults use Invisalign too, since the trays are hard to spot at work and in photos. Teens, adults, and older adults can all be a good fit, if their teeth and bite suit the trays. The team will check at the scan visit.

What happens when I finish Invisalign?

After the last tray, you get a retainer to hold your teeth in their new spots. You wear it as the team tells you, often at night, so the teeth do not drift back. Come in for checkups so the team can check the bite and the retainer fit.

What if I lose a tray?

Call the office right away and do not skip ahead to the next tray without asking. The team may tell you to wear the last tray, move to the next one, or come in for a new tray, based on how long you wore the lost one.
